CodeShift Keyboard — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction and Your Consent

myGIGlife INC. ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") publishes CodeShift Keyboard (the "App"), an on-screen keyboard for Android with a standard typing mode and a coding mode. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect, how the information it handles is used and stored, which third parties are involved, and your rights with respect to that information.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. This Privacy Policy forms part of the App's Terms of Use. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

2. Definitions

In this Privacy Policy:
"Personal information" (called "personal data" in some laws) means information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with a particular person, directly or indirectly, such as a name, an email address, or an IP address.
"Device" means the phone or tablet on which you use the App.
"App Store" means the Google Play Store.
"Third-party service" means a service that is not operated by us, such as the speech recognition service, Google Play, and the apps you type into, as described below.

3. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. A keyboard handles everything you type, so we want to be direct: what you type is processed on your device only and goes to the app you are typing in. The App has no user accounts, logins, or passwords, does not ask for your name or email address, and has no servers of its own.

The App does not have permission to use the internet. It cannot send what you type, your clipboard, or anything else off your device. We do not collect, log, or monitor device identifiers, advertising identifiers, contacts, photos, location, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other data from your device or about you. The App does not use cookies, sessions, tracking pixels, or similar tracking technologies, shows no advertising, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

Section 4 describes the optional microphone key. Sections 5 and 6 describe information the App keeps on your device. Section 7 describes what we receive if you choose to email us.

4. Voice Input and the Microphone

The App asks for the Android microphone permission only so the microphone key can work, and only when you first tap that key. The App never listens in the background, and voice input is off unless you tap the key.

When you use voice input, your audio is handled by a speech recognition service that you already have on your device, not by us. Depending on your setting, the microphone key either hands dictation to Google voice typing, which opens in place of the keyboard, or uses the speech recognition service built into Android. Either service is operated by its provider (usually Google), may process your audio on its servers, and is governed by its own terms and privacy policy, not by this Privacy Policy. The App does not receive, record, or store your audio. It receives only the text the service returns and inserts it into the field you are typing in.

Voice input is optional. You can refuse or revoke the microphone permission at any time in your device's settings, and every other part of the App works without it.

5. Information Stored on Your Device

To work well, the App saves the following on your device, using your device operating system's standard local app storage:

- your settings, such as keyboard size, theme, layout, vibration, and typing options;
- the keyboard mode you last used in each app (stored with that app's package name), so the App can open in the same mode next time;
- words you choose to save to your dictionary with the check mark in the suggestion bar;
- pairs of words learned from your typing, used to suggest the next word, which are not saved while Incognito mode is on, in password fields, or in fields that ask keyboards not to learn;
- text you pin in the clipboard panel, which stays until you unpin it; and
- for support, the most recent crash report and a short log of voice input events, which you can view and copy in the App's settings. These contain technical details and do not contain what you typed.

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us, to an App Store, or to any other third party, and we have no access to it, no ability to retrieve it, and no copy of it. The App turns off Android cloud backup, so it is not copied to your Google account either.

You can remove saved words and pinned text in the App, and you can delete everything the App keeps by clearing the App's data in your device's settings or by uninstalling the App.

6. Clipboard and Password Fields

The App keeps a short history of items you copy so you can paste them again, and shows the newest one as a paste suggestion in the bar above the keys. This history is kept in the device's memory only, is lost when the keyboard process ends, and can be cleared at any time from the App's settings. Text you pin is stored as described in Section 5.

The App does not keep clipboard items, learn words, or show suggestions in password fields. Incognito mode, which you can turn on from the bar above the keys or in settings, stops the App from learning words or remembering clipboard items, and apps can request it for private tabs and chats.

7. Emails You Choose to Send Us

The App never sends anything to us by itself. If you contact us, or copy a crash report from the App's settings and send it to us, we receive what your email contains: your name and email address, your message, and anything you chose to paste into it.

We use an email only to read and answer it, to fix problems it reports, and to improve the App, and we keep it only as long as needed for those purposes or as required by law. We do not add you to any mailing list. You can ask us to delete an email you have sent us, as described in Section 18.
