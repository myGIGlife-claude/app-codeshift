CodeShift Keyboard

A single, private keyboard for everything you type. A coding keyboard for terminals and editors and a smart everyday keyboard for chat and email in one Android app, with glide typing, voice input, a clipboard with pinned snippets, and English, Spanish and German. One-time purchase. No ads, no tracking, no account, and no internet permission, so the keyboard cannot send what you type anywhere. Voice input is optional and is handled by your device's speech service (usually Google), which may process the audio; CodeShift never receives it.
